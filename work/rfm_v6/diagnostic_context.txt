Full-annulus Re=100 shedding diagnostics
--------------------------------------
Lengths are scaled by D=1 and velocities by the final streamwise inflow
U=1. The domain is a circular annulus, a=0.5 and R=6. A common temporary
transverse boundary pulse, 0.12 sin^4(pi*(t-4)/8) for 4<t<12, breaks
reflection while permitting an unforced evolution after t=12.

At theta=0, the transverse velocity is the polar azimuthal velocity.
Centreline probes are at x/D=1.5, 2.5, and 3.5. For t>=36, the numerical
diagnostic counts positive-going crossings around the median, demands at
least three complete periods, opposite raw signs, persistent amplitude,
period CV at most 0.10, and frequency concordance (within 0.10) for
velocity and vorticity at two or more locations. The spectral peak is a
secondary check. Relative downstream phases are computed by fitting a
common harmonic to the time traces and can indicate downstream
propagation. The diagnostic also reports an approximate saturation test.

For reflection in y, u_r is even, u_theta is odd, and vorticity is odd.
The square norm of the forbidden component is computed by Parseval:
mean and real Fourier parts measure even content, while imaginary
positive modes measure odd content. This use of a reflection is only
an observation; the discretization advances both parity sectors.

A spatial confirmation compares wake vorticity at times separated by
P/2 with the reflected and sign-reversed original field, and at P
with the original field. The wake cut is 1.5<=x/D<=4.5,
|y|/D<=1.25. Relative errors are weighted by physical area. Repeating
this test on each eligible snapshot separates a typical cycle property
from a single attractive image. Time interpolation is only applied to
this physical diagnostic; exact-time benchmark field errors remain
unshifted. Upper-minus-lower enstrophy is also reported as a dimensionless
imbalance. Alternating signed vorticity, agreement of the frequencies,
and the half-cycle reflection in space collectively support shedding.

Fine FD diagnostic results
--------------------------
The cold n=200, k=144, rate=256, outer-cosine trajectory gives
P=5.696686 and St=0.1755406 for the window 36<=t<=72. Adjacent
downstream vorticity phase delays are 1.368 and 1.487 D/U. The
three transverse-velocity period CVs are about 0.05% to 0.10%.
The corresponding late/early per-cycle amplitude ratios are
1.008, 1.005, and 1.001.

For an independent physical visualization, t=60..66 states at spacing
0.25 were regenerated by restarting that trajectory at t=54. Restart
state differences relative to the cold run at t=60 and 66 are 3.24e-7
and 2.80e-7. The restarted states are solely for the visualization and
the spatial half-cycle diagnostic. The plotted probe trace and period
come from the untouched cold-start trajectory.

The 13 half-cycle comparisons have median relative wake-vorticity
differences 0.02493 after spatial reflection and sign reversal and
1.42100 without either. The two full-period comparisons give 0.01835.
The normalized upper-minus-lower wake enstrophy ranges from -0.17374
to 0.17335. The normalized reflection-breaking vorticity norm is
0.39095 to 0.41808 across the 25 snapshots. Interpolated comparisons
are illustrative and do not enter the exact-time numerical gates.
