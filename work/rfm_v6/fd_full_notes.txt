Full-annulus fourth-order finite-difference reference

Geometry and forcing follow full_case.py. Azimuthal modes are stored
as the nonnegative normalized real-Fourier coefficients. For k>0
the state is the interior streamfunction, with prescribed values at
both boundaries. The inner slope is zero and the inner vorticity is
reconstructed by a Hermite formula using the wall value and slope
and four interior nodes. Outer vorticity is zero, as in the Galerkin
experiment. Complex coefficients allow the cosine and sine branches.

Mean mode
---------
Incompressibility and zero net radial flux give mean u_r=0. The
azimuthal component of the rotational velocity equation is

  d_t v = -<u_r omega> + nu d_r omega_0,
  omega_0 = (v_s+v)/r, s=log(r/a).

Azimuthal pressure and kinetic-energy gradients vanish on averaging.
The zero mode uses v directly, so the calculation can generate
nonzero circulation. The viscous term is nu*(v_ss-v)/r^2. Enforce
v=0 at the inner wall and v_s+v=0 at the outer wall. The outer Robin
value is eliminated with the five-point one-sided derivative stencil.
Mean nonlinear forcing is evaluated with Fourier Parseval, including
both members of each Hermitian conjugate pair.

Nonzero modes
-------------
The interior curl uses omega_k=-(D_ss-k^2) psi_k/r^2. The same
stencils act on omega to produce the diffusion operator. The
advective form is -(u_r omega_s+u_theta omega_theta)/r at every
interior node. Exact angular de-aliasing uses N>3K. The FD and
Galerkin nonlinear forms are equivalent in the continuum; FD does
not inherit the weak Galerkin cancellation at finite radial spacing.
The standard v6 driver starts from rest and advances with SBDF2.

Grid and practical convergence
------------------------------
The original inner-clustered FD grid is unsuitable for precision
scoring of late shedding. At the outer downstream boundary the
imposed zero vorticity produces a thin viscous layer. Outer cosine
stretching s/L=sin(pi*t/2), t in [0,1], improves outflow resolution.
Both-wall cosine and smoothly inner-clustered configurations are
also retained as diagnostics. The radial stencil audit measures
convergence for analytic wall-compatible functions; it does not
guarantee fourth-order trajectory accuracy for every flow quantity.

Native band accelerator
-----------------------
The Python setup builds each radial band operator, factors it with
LAPACK DGBTRF and passes cached factors and pivots to fd_full_band.c.
The C routine performs pivoted substitution on both real and
imaginary RHS. This avoids Python calls for every angular mode.
Use the shipped x64 DLL on Windows or rebuild with a 64-bit MinGW:

  x86_64-w64-mingw32-gcc -std=c99 -O3 -shared -nostdlib -nodefaultlibs -Wl,--entry,0 -o fd_full_band.dll fd_full_band.c

On Linux use gcc -std=c99 -O3 -shared -fPIC -o fd_full_band.so fd_full_band.c.
SciPy backend linear='scipy' uses the same factors and provides a
portable numerical check, with a different runtime. fd_full_audit.py
checks the factor application and precomputed probes. FD timing
includes factor assembly and setup, regardless of linear backend.

Dense spatial diagnostic
------------------------
fd_full_dense_from54.npz restarts the fine FD200 run at its t=54 state,
does one backward-Euler step, and then uses SBDF2. It stores late
quarter-time-unit frames only for wake diagnostics, not accuracy
scores or timing. Its probe histories are taken from the untouched
cold-start source. The checkpoint-state relative differences from
the original solve are 3.24e-7 at t=60 and 2.80e-7 at t=66.
