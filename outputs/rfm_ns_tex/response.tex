\noindent\textbf{Revision summary.}
The revision preserves the original 78 GPU qualification trajectories and rejected timing records. It adds 20 complete CPU trajectories, two error-decomposition figures, and a maintained explicit-basis-factory interface with verification. We address the requested ablations, geometric scope, error distributions, switch sensitivity, and maintainability, while correcting parameter, formula, and degree-of-freedom transcriptions that conflict with the archived source. \section{主要意见1：随机频率与解析层函数的消融}
\textbf{修改与证据。}
新增两档 $T=72$ 两因素对照：$(m,K,q,\Delta t^{-1})=(37,72,66,128)$ 与 $(41,80,76,152)$；频率采用五个注册随机种子或固定中点 $\xi_j=0.5$，非零模六个解析层函数开/关。均值块始终保持同一30维构造。新跑14条消融轨迹及2条CPU复算控制，复用并重评分10条冻结GPU控制。每条轨迹在19个物理时刻、3套重构网格、沿角向与时间细化的2套FD参考上检查全域和近场的速度、涡量四指标。

固定中点并保留层函数时，两档最坏全域涡量误差分别约 $0.4092\%$、$0.3312\%$，均通过 $0.5\%$ 场门槛，与随机频率有层控制相当。去掉非零模层函数的12条轨迹均运行完成，却全部不满足精度，最坏全域涡量约 $71.85\%$--$73.90\%$。故当前证据不支持随机抖动是达到该门槛的必要条件。

\textbf{限制。}移除层函数也使保留秩由39/40降至34/35，不能把误差变化解释为同维数下纯粹的层函数形状效应。进一步固定带宽62和截断阈值，检查 $m=41,45,49,53,57,61$，两档无层空间的秩均为35、35、36、36、36、36，未直接恢复39/40。该廉价构造检查不增加PDE轨迹，也不替代更广的等秩、变带宽研究。摘要、引言及讨论据此收紧随机性贡献表述。

\noindent\textbf{English response.}
We added a two-factor ablation at two resolutions, comparing five registered random jitters with fixed frequency midpoints and toggling the six nonzero-mode analytic layers. The 30-dimensional mean block is held fixed. Fourteen new ablation runs and two CPU controls supplement ten frozen GPU controls; all are rescored at 19 times on three grids against two angular/time-refined FD references using four field metrics. Both midpoint-plus-layer configurations pass, with worst full-domain vorticity errors of approximately $0.4092\%$ and $0.3312\%$. All 12 no-layer trajectories complete but fail accuracy. Randomness is therefore not necessary for the tested tolerance within these enriched configurations. Removal also reduces rank from 39/40 to 34/35, preventing an equal-rank causal attribution. Increasing raw size at fixed bandwidth and cutoff produces ranks at most 36 in the added construction checks. We retain this limitation and do not assign a numerical percentage of the benefit to enrichment or randomness.

\section{主要意见2：规则几何与推广边界}
\textbf{修改与证据。}
新增非可分几何讨论，区分隐式线性模态分块和仍存在的非线性模态耦合。当前每步主成本为 $O(Kd^2+qdK+qN\log N)$，模态稠密准备为 $O(Kd^3)$；角度相关度量会破坏独立模态分解。若朴素地改为 $D=O(Kd)$ 的全局稠密系统，则存储和分解为 $O(D^2)$、$O(D^3)$，但局部稀疏、无矩阵和区域分解方法可以避免该朴素成本。新增条件性谱等价界明确哪些常数必须重新控制，并引用复杂界面RFM的原始研究，避免把已有线性Stokes证据移植成本文非稳态NS结论。

\textbf{对建议措辞的调整。}我们没有采用“证明RFM无法击败谱方法与高阶差分”的表述。最终完整计时未获接受，既不能建立稳定胜出，也不能证明性能劣于最强对照。修订稿仍表述为“在指定有限圆环及当前证据下，整体速度优势尚未建立”。

\noindent\textbf{English response.}
The added geometric-scope subsection explains the modal factorization, its cost, and its loss under angle-dependent metrics. A fully dense coupled solve is presented as an avoidable design choice, not an inherent lower bound for irregular domains. A conditional spectral-equivalence bound states the assumptions required for a reference-operator preconditioner. We cite primary interface-RFM research while distinguishing its linear examples from the present nonlinear evolution. We do not adopt the proposed wording that RFM has been proved unable to beat classical methods: the rejected final timing campaign establishes neither a win nor a loss. The conclusion remains a scoped absence of established advantage.

\section{主要意见3：速度与涡量误差排序的差别}
\textbf{修改与证据。}
新增径向误差剖面、角模误差能量谱、同一物理时刻的对比以及各指标极值时刻。图中径向误差以全域参考RMS归一化，角谱以全域参考平方范数归一化，避免参考局部过零导致比例发散。380个Parseval积分与原重构网格评分的最大差为 $2.13\times10^{-17}$。

例如统一 $t=48$ 的高档分段控制中，Legendre速度误差约 $0.01842\%$，RFM为 $0.01886\%$；涡量则分别为 $0.35509\%$、$0.29438\%$。在 $t=72$，FD速度误差平方能量约 $84.24\%$ 位于 $k\leq8$；RFM涡量误差约 $47.72\%$ 位于 $25\leq k\leq80$。这支持速度与导数误差受不同成分影响，但不是对误差来源的唯一因果证明。RFM在 $r\geq4$ 的涡量误差能量占约 $61.14\%$，而极薄外层 $r\geq5.9$ 仅占 $1.37\%$，不能将全部误差归于薄边界层。上述RFM剖面明确使用种子7331；跨种子最坏表仍单独保留。

\noindent\textbf{English response.}
Two new figures and underlying data quantify radial and angular error distributions at common physical times. Global-reference normalization avoids divisions by vanishing local fields. The numerical Parseval checks agree with the reconstructed-grid scores to $2.13\times10^{-17}$. The common-time comparisons retain a velocity/vorticity ranking reversal, while the modal and radial energy fractions identify different error distributions. These are descriptive diagnostics rather than a unique causal decomposition. The original maxima occur at different times and sometimes different grids or seeds; the revision no longer interprets them as simultaneous flow-state comparisons or attributes all error to an extremely thin layer.

\section{主要意见4：启动段与延后换步}
\textbf{修改与证据。}
在两档RFM和Legendre空间各新增一条 $t_s=12$ 的CPU轨迹，共4条；其余参数保持不变。恒步与 $t_s=8$ 控制来自冻结记录。新增轨迹在 $t\leq12$ 的保存状态与对应恒步控制逐元素相同。四条均通过四个场指标及相对恒步的逐格三角保护，但改善有限。中档RFM最大速度误差从 $0.044672\%$ 降至 $0.043821\%$，涡量从 $0.431680\%$ 降至 $0.429910\%$，仍未回到恒步水平。每条轨迹多64次非线性评估；本次没有测量延后换步的GPU完整耗时。

\textbf{解释修正。}原 $10^{-5}$ 门槛检查的是同一离散方案的CPU/native/GPU实现差异，不是允许变更时间策略的物理误差门槛。$0.02943\%$ 到 $0.04467\%$ 是约 $51.8\%$ 相对增长、$0.01524$ 个百分点，不能由这两个极值推出桥接导致降阶。$t=12$ 处脉冲及其前三阶导数连续，但第四阶导数跳跃，移到该点也不能不加假设地套用跨点四阶光滑性。修订将本实验称为换步敏感性，而非收敛阶验证。

\noindent\textbf{English response.}
Four new $t_s=12$ trajectories compare the same mid/high RFM and Legendre spaces with their archived constant-step and $t_s=8$ controls. All pass the field and cellwise triangle safeguards, but the improvement is modest and does not recover constant-step accuracy. Each incurs 64 additional nonlinear evaluations. No GPU timing benefit is inferred. We distinguish the $10^{-5}$ same-discretization implementation tolerance from the physical schedule comparison, percentage growth from percentage-point changes, and sensitivity from convergence-order evidence. The pulse is $C^3$ at its endpoint, with a fourth-derivative jump, so a switch at 12 does not automatically eliminate all regularity concerns.

\section{细节意见2：准备开销的交叉点}
\textbf{修改与证据。}
新增显式条件模型。令额外准备 $\Delta P=0.206$--$0.240$ 秒，历史推进节省 $\Delta M_0=0.119$ 秒，候选总步数 $N_0=8192$，不变启动段 $N_s=1024$。仅让后段节省线性增长时，
\[
 \Delta T(N)=\Delta P-\frac{N-N_s}{N_0-N_s}\Delta M_0,
 \qquad N_*=N_s+(N_0-N_s)\frac{\Delta P}{\Delta M_0}.
\]
对应约13,432--15,480候选步。粗略按全部步数缩放则约14,181--16,522步。两种计算均来自标记为 \texttt{diagnostic\_only} 的非隔离记录，仅说明模型假设，不是已验证约15,000步后胜出。更长时域仍须验证精度；多轨迹共享准备也须实际实现，不能由PTX缓存直接推断。

\noindent\textbf{English response.}
The revision gives the requested crossover formula, explicitly distinguishing a late-work model from an all-step approximation. Both yield a planning scale near $1.5\times10^4$ steps, but neither is a measured threshold. The input timing window was diagnostic and nonisolated, longer-horizon accuracy is untested, and repeated solves amortize preparation only if the relevant objects are truly shared. We retain these conditions next to the estimate.

\section{细节意见3：移除运行时全局替换}
\textbf{修改与证据。}
审稿指出的问题确实存在于旧稳态控制，但实际替换对象是 \path{rfm_v2_solver.trial_basis}，不属于最终圆环路径。为保留冻结证据，我们在独立维护包中添加每次求解局部传入的 \texttt{basis\_factory}，使省略参数的旧默认调用保持行为；映射Legendre包装器直接传工厂，不再修改模块全局状态。没有覆盖历史源码，也没有把这项维护描述为圆环GPU加速。

全部7项CPU测试通过。8种小配置与旧实现比较系数、Stokes矩阵、25个独立点的速度、涡量等字段及历史记录，采用相对和绝对容差 $10^{-12}$。其他测试强制不同工厂并发重叠，检查工厂异常、组装异常以及一个线程失败后另一个求解不受污染。验证范围是此次接口兼容和状态隔离，不保证任意外部自定义工厂都具备线程安全性。

\noindent\textbf{English response.}
We replaced the early steady-solver wrapper's global \texttt{trial\_basis} patch with a solve-local explicit factory in a maintained package, preserving default calls and frozen historical files. Seven CPU tests pass, including eight legacy-equivalence cases, deliberately overlapping factories, and exception isolation. This is a real maintainability correction, not a new annular solver optimization or a guarantee about arbitrary user factories. The maintained API, command, source hashes, and actual test receipt accompany the revision.
